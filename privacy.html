Privacy Policy
Your privacy is our top priority
Last updated: June 2026
🛡️ My AI Curator stores ALL data locally on your device only.
We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
1. Overview

My AI Curator ("the Extension") is a privacy-first Chrome extension. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. The short version: we don't collect anything from you.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

My AI Curator does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Browsing history or search queries
  • Location data
  • Financial information
  • Login credentials or passwords
  • Any data to external servers
3. Data Stored Locally

The Extension stores the following data locally in your browser only:

  • Selected topics and keywords you choose
  • Browsing activity data (searches, YouTube views, shopping) — stored only on your device
  • Tracker blocking statistics
  • Extension settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device and is only accessible by you.

4. Tracker Blocking

The Extension blocks third-party trackers to protect your privacy. This blocking happens entirely on your device. No information about blocked trackers is sent to us or any third party.

5. Permissions Used
  • storage — To save your preferences and data locally
  • declarativeNetRequest — To block trackers at the network level
  • cookies — To remove tracking cookies from your browser
  • tabs — To show per-page tracker blocking statistics
6. Third-Party Services

My AI Curator does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The web app (my-curator.vercel.app) uses Google Analytics only on the web app, not in the extension.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by going to Settings → Clear All Data within the Extension. Uninstalling the Extension also removes all stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

My AI Curator is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
