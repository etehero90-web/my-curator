Introduction
What is My AI Curator?
A privacy-first Chrome extension that keeps your browsing data on your device — no servers, no tracking, no accounts required.
🛡️
Block 800+ Trackers
Network-level blocking of ad, analytics, and social trackers
Automatically blocks over 800 known tracking domains including ad networks, analytics tools, and social tracking pixels. Pages load faster too.
📊
Collect Your Browsing Data (Locally)
Searches, YouTube, shopping, streaming — stored only on your device
Your Google searches, YouTube views, shopping activity, and streaming history are stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever sent to any server.
💎
See Your Data's Value
Find out how much your browsing profile is worth to advertisers
Based on real advertising industry CPM rates, My AI Curator estimates what your browsing data is worth in dollars. In the future, you'll be able to sell it directly.
All data stays on your device. No servers. No tracking. No account needed. Completely free.
Installation
How to Install
Available for free on the Chrome Web Store.
1
Open the Chrome Web Store
Search for "My AI Curator" in the Chrome Web Store.
2
Click "Add to Chrome"
Click the "Add to Chrome" button and accept the permissions.
3
Pin the Extension
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your toolbar → find My AI Curator → click the pin icon for easy access.
4
Start Using It
Tracker blocking starts immediately. Visit any website and click the icon to see what's being blocked!
When prompted for permissions, click "Allow" to enable tracker blocking and data collection features.

How Value is Calculated
CPM-based calculation
Uses real advertising industry CPM (cost per 1,000 users) rates. Finance ($12.5), Automotive ($11.0), Travel ($9.0) etc.
Purchase intent weighting
Cart adds (×10) > Product views (×4) > Shopping searches (×3) > General searches (×1). The closer to a purchase, the more valuable.
TOP 3 categories
Your three most valuable data categories shown with gold, silver, and bronze medals.
Intent level
HIGH / MED / LOW indicates how much advertiser demand exists for each category based on your activity volume.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Is my data sent to any server?
No. All data is stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever transmitted to any external server.
Will tracker blocking break websites?
Most websites work fine. In rare cases where a site breaks, you can temporarily disable blocking in Settings, or add a domain exception via the Custom Block Domains setting.
Is the data value ($) real money I can receive?
Not yet. The current value is an estimate based on advertising industry CPM rates. Direct data selling is planned for a future version (v4.0) of the Data Marketplace feature.
Will my data disappear if I switch browsers?
Yes. Data is stored in your current browser only. Use the Export tab to save a JSON backup before switching.
What happens to my data if I uninstall the extension?
All stored data will be permanently deleted along with the extension. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.
Is there a mobile version?
An Android app is coming soon and is currently in closed beta testing. Follow us on Bluesky for updates.
How do I add more sites to block?
Go to Settings → Custom Block Domains and enter the domain you want to block (e.g. ads.example.com). It will be added on top of the existing 777 blocked domains.
