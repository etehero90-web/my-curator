Introduction
What is My AI Curator?
A privacy-first Chrome extension that keeps your browsing data on your device — no servers, no tracking, no accounts required.
🛡️
Block 800+ Trackers
Network-level blocking of ad, analytics, and social trackers
Automatically blocks over 800 known tracking domains including ad networks, analytics tools, and social tracking pixels. Pages load faster too.
📊
Collect Your Browsing Data (Locally)
Searches, YouTube, shopping, streaming — stored only on your device
Your Google searches, YouTube views, shopping activity, and streaming history are stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever sent to any server.
💎
See Your Data's Value
Find out how much your browsing profile is worth to advertisers
Based on real advertising industry CPM rates, My AI Curator estimates what your browsing data is worth in dollars — boosted by behavioral signals like purchase intent.
All data stays on your device. No servers. No tracking. No account needed. Completely free.
Installation
How to Install
Available for free on the Chrome Web Store.
1
Open the Chrome Web Store
Search for "My AI Curator" in the Chrome Web Store.
2
Click "Add to Chrome"
Click the "Add to Chrome" button and accept the permissions.
3
Pin the Extension
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your toolbar → find My AI Curator → click the pin icon for easy access.
4
Start Using It
Tracker blocking starts immediately. Visit any website and click the icon to see what's being blocked!
When prompted for permissions, click "Allow" to enable tracker blocking and data collection features.

🎯 Your Signals
Beyond simple category tracking, My AI Curator detects behavioral signals that make your data more (or less) valuable — the same way real advertisers evaluate audiences.
🔥 High Purchase Intent
Detected when you search or view the same category 3+ times within 3 days. Signals you're close to making a purchase decision.
🛒 Cart Abandonment
Detected when items sit in your cart for 24+ hours without purchase. One of the strongest purchase-intent signals advertisers look for.
📈 Rising / New Interest
Detected when a category shows a new or 50%+ increase in activity compared to last week — signals a shift in what you care about.
📉 Declining Interest
Detected when a category drops 50%+ compared to last week.
💔 Churn Risk
Detected when you search things like "cancel Netflix" or "alternative to Spotify" — signals you might be switching services.
💎 Value Tier (Premium / Budget)
Detected from the brands you search — premium brands (Chanel, Rolex...) vs budget brands (Daiso, clearance sales...).
Signals are calculated 100% locally on your device. They only affect your estimated data value display — nothing is sent anywhere.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Is my data sent to any server?
No. All data, including signal calculations, is stored and processed only in your browser's local storage.
Where did the weekly report and duplicate cleaner go?
We simplified the Overview tab. The weekly report moved to My Value, and duplicate cleanup moved to Settings → Data Collection Status.
Will tracker blocking break websites?
Most websites work fine. You can temporarily disable blocking in Settings, or add a domain exception.
Is the data value ($) real money I can receive?
Not yet. It's an estimate based on advertising industry CPM rates, boosted by behavioral signals. Direct data selling is planned for a future Data Marketplace (v4.0).
Will my data disappear if I switch browsers?
Yes. Data is stored in your current browser only. Use the Export tab to save a backup before switching.
Is there a mobile version?
Yes! An Android app is in public beta testing. Check the extension header for the latest link.
